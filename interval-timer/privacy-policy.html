Privacy policy

Interval Timer: Fully Moldable · Last updated 24 November 2026

Interval Timer: Fully Moldable ("the app") is an interval timer. It works entirely on your device. This policy explains what that means for your information.

What the app collects

The app itself collects nothing: it has no account and no analytics, and your workouts, settings and history never leave your device. We (the developer) receive no personal information.

The version from Google Play shows a few ads to pay for its development, and those are provided by Google (see "Ads" below). The version from itch.io has no ads and no internet access at all.

Ads (Google Play version)

Ads are kept rare: one small card among the presets and, at most once every five finished sessions, a full-screen ad when you tap Finish. There are never ads during a workout. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. It is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google's use is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

  • Your choice: where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) the app asks for your consent before ads are personalised, and you can change your answer at any time under Options → Privacy & ad choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Remove ads: where a one-time Remove ads purchase is offered in Options, it turns every ad off. The purchase is handled by Google Play; we never see your payment details.

What stays on your device

  • Your presets, chains, settings and theme.
  • Your session history (date, length and preset name), used for the History screen, weekly goal and streak.
  • Sound files you choose for alerts: the app keeps permission to play the file you picked; it does not copy or upload it.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this.

Backups and sharing you start

  • Backup file: if you use "Back up", the app writes a file to the place you choose. Where that file goes afterwards is up to you.
  • Sharing a preset: if you use "Share", the app hands a short text code to the app you pick (for example a chat app).
  • Android backup: if Android's own backup is turned on, Android may include the app's settings in your device backup with Google. This is handled by Android and your Google account, not by the app.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the running timer and let you pause or stop it from the notification.
  • Foreground service (media playback): so alerts and the voice coach keep playing with the screen off.
  • Alarms and reminders: to schedule each alert at the exact second, even if the phone pauses the app.
  • Vibration and keep awake: for vibration alerts and accurate timing while a session runs.
  • Internet, network state, advertising ID and billing (Google Play version only): to load ads, to respect your ad choices, and for the optional Remove ads purchase where it is offered.
  • Display over other apps (optional, off unless you turn it on): only for Edge lights → Over other apps, to show the alert light around the screen's edge while another app is open. The light is see-through, never takes touches and reads nothing from the screen.

The voice coach uses your phone's text-to-speech engine. That engine is part of Android or an app you installed, and follows its own privacy terms.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
